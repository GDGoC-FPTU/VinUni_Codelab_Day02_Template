# Phase 1 Problem Scan and AI Log Design

## Scope

- Update `01-problem-scan.md` Phase 1 only.
- Keep Phase 2 Quick Problem Cards blank.
- Add `03-ai-log.md` documenting the ChatGPT Web research and the subsequent source recheck.

## Phase 1 content

The scan will contain five Xanh SM operational opportunities:

1. Customer-support escalation copilot.
2. Payment and reconciliation exception analysis.
3. Vehicle handover photo inspection.
4. Maintenance triage and scheduling.
5. Fleet charging coordination.

Each row will identify the relevant lens and describe the operational bottleneck. Public facts and estimates will be separated explicitly. Calculated annual values will be labeled as scenario assumptions, not confirmed Green SM losses or savings.

The scan will account for existing Green SM capabilities found during verification, including V-App charging-route/session features and online maintenance booking. Therefore, the opportunities will focus on exceptions, post-checking, triage, or fleet-level optimization rather than claiming those existing features do not exist.

## AI log content

`03-ai-log.md` will record:

- The original brainstorming prompt and intended use.
- What the AI contributed.
- Which claims required caution or correction.
- Source verification results and access limitations.
- Changes made to the final Phase 1 scan.
- Reflection on AI as a thought partner.

## Verification criteria

- The scan has exactly five populated Phase 1 rows.
- Each row includes a subsidiary, one of the worksheet lenses, and a concrete bottleneck.
- No estimate is presented as an audited internal Green SM metric.
- Existing official product capabilities are not misrepresented.
- Phase 2 remains unfilled.
- The AI log identifies sources and uncertainty.
